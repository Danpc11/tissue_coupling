# Data

## Public downloads expected under `data/raw/`

Nineteen GEO series and GTEx v11. Accessions, tissue, platform, sample number and the role each
series plays are listed in Supplementary Table S1 of the manuscript:

GSE11868, GSE13070, GSE17992, GSE25462, GSE26637, GSE41168, GSE66446, GSE78721, GSE87761,
GSE95674, GSE95675, GSE104674, GSE107537, GSE108539, GSE129843, GSE148640, GSE159984, GSE182117,
GSE182120.

GSE107537 is the external control for the estimator: a controlled 10-h delay of the habitual sleep
period with recorded sampling times, whose published result is dampened amplitude at a preserved
phase. Its platform annotation (GPL24324) is needed to resolve transcript cluster identifiers to
gene symbols.

GSE95674 and GSE95675 are the two tissues of one study (AAGMEx). GSE182117 and GSE182120 are
separate studies despite adjacent accessions.

For GTEx, the gene-reads GCT of each tissue plus the subject-phenotype and sample-attribute
annotation files. Individual-level controlled-access data require dbGaP authorisation; the
analyses here use the open-access expression matrices and annotations.

The DoRothEA A–C regulons (`dorothea_ABC.tsv`, exported from OmniPath) are also expected under
`data/raw/`. Platform annotation files for the microarray series, and the NCBI gene annotation used to resolve
Entrez identifiers to current symbols, are also expected here. `00_prepare_data.py` lists each file
it needs and fails with the missing name rather than silently skipping it.

## Versioned inputs under `data/reference/`

These were inputs that `run_all.sh` did not regenerate. On the branch `repro-hugo` every one of them is regenerated by `00d_reference_tables.py` and compared with the versioned copy; the copies stay here only as the reference for that comparison.

| file | why it is versioned |
|---|---|
| `GSE13070_IR_vs_sensitive_log2.tsv` | the insulin-resistant against insulin-sensitive contrast of GSE13070 is unbalanced (35 against 6 in adipose tissue) and was not reproduced consistently; it is used only to define a profile of change compared with other profiles, and is excluded from the differential expression of Fig. 1 |
| `signatures_reference.tsv` | the twelve-contrast signature table. `23_build_signatures_table.py` regenerates three of its columns from public data and **checks each against this reference**, aborting if any disagrees; the rest are carried over |
| `template_2harm.tsv` | the first- and second-harmonic template, derived from cohorts with recorded sampling times |
| the remaining files | per-cohort intermediates that a single step produces and several figures read; versioned so a figure can be redrawn without rerunning the whole pipeline |

## Convention

Scores computed across individuals use per-sample centred expression. Differences between groups,
including the signature table, are computed without centring. See the README.

## Reconstruction of the preparation step (branch `repro-hugo`, November 2026)

The published package did not include the step that turns the public downloads into the matrices under `results/new/*.pkl`. This branch adds it:

| script | what it produces |
|---|---|
| `download_public.sh` | every public input listed above, from GEO FTP, the GTEx bucket, Broad, OmniPath and recount3; `T2D_ARCHS4=1` also fetches the ARCHS4 HDF5 |
| `python/analyses/00b_build_matrices.py` | the 26 `*.pkl` matrices (annotation by Entrez for the Affymetrix platforms, by platform symbol plus NCBI synonyms for Illumina HT-12 whose GEO table has no Entrez column, Ensembl-to-current symbol for RNA-seq and GTEx; log2 CPM with the library size taken before gene filtering; one donor per column for GTEx, the sample that SampleAttributes lists first), the `*_meta.pkl` tables, the NCBI synonym map and, as a fallback, `GSE108539_counts.tsv.gz` from recount3 (coverage sums divided by read length) |
| `python/analyses/02e_gse87761_benchmark.py` | infers the phase of the 33 GSE87761 adipose samples with the muscle template and compares it with the recorded sampling time (`results/new/gse87761_benchmark.tsv`) |
| `python/analyses/05_clamp_within_person.py` (block E) | per-person change of the clock-output score under the clamp, insulin-sensitive against insulin-resistant, in GSE13070 muscle and GSE26637 adipose tissue, with the two cohorts combined (`results/new/clamp_response_by_sensitivity.tsv`) |
| `python/analyses/00d_reference_tables.py` | regenerates the 23 tables of `data/reference/` that had no producer, writes them to `results/new/` and compares each with the versioned copy (`results/new/reference_tables_check.tsv`) |
| `python/analyses/01c_islets_deseq2.py` | `GSE159984_islets_T2D_vs_ND.tsv`, the PyDESeq2 contrast of the 85 islet donors (27 T2D against 58 non-diabetic) that Fig. 1b-c reads |
| `python/analyses/00c_archs4_counts.py` | `GSE159984_raw_counts.tsv.gz` from ARCHS4, read by HTTP range if the HDF5 is not on disk |

Substitutes for files that are only served by `www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov`, which answers non-interactive clients with a CAPTCHA:

- `Human_GRCh38_p13_annot_tsv.gz` is derived from `Homo_sapiens.gene_info.gz` (NCBI Gene FTP); the scripts read only `GeneID` and `Symbol`.
- The NCBI-generated counts (saved as `GSE108539_raw_counts_GRCh38_p13_NCBI.tsv.gz` and `GSE159984_raw_counts_GRCh38_p13_NCBI.tsv.gz`, underscores, with `GSE159984_raw_counts.tsv.gz` as a link to the latter) download with a plain `curl` when NCBI is not rate-limiting the client; when it answers with a CAPTCHA page instead, `00b_build_matrices.py` falls back to recount3 for GSE108539 (SRP127581) and `00c_archs4_counts.py` can supply the 44 cultured preparations of GSE159984 from ARCHS4 (the 86 donor islets, GPL9115, are not in ARCHS4 nor recount3).
- Platform tables (GPL570, GPL15207, GPL17586, GPL24324) are the `!platform_table` block of the family SOFT file, read in streaming mode.
- CHIRAL (step 06) needs the Python port by Andrea Salati, which is not on PyPI: `git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/AndreaSalati/pyCHIRAL.git` (commit e1ea76e was used) and `PYCHIRAL_SRC=<clone>/src`, which `run_all.sh` defaults to `~/circadian_timing/external/pyCHIRAL/src`; it also needs `tqdm`. Without it step 06 prints a warning and skips the concordance.
- `gene_info` and the OmniPath export have no version; `download_public.sh` appends their date and sha256 to `data/raw/CHECKSUMS.txt`.
- GSE78721 has 130 samples from 95 people (35 abdominal-surgery patients contribute subcutaneous and visceral depots; GEO gives no person identifier). The branch uses one sample per person (limb or subcutaneous) in the case-control analysis and in the meta-analysis, with the depot as a covariate.
- GSE129843 is used from the author's CPM table (no raw counts deposited); genes with CPM > 1 in at least 10 samples.

`GSE148640_processed.txt.gz`, the processed supplement of that series, is also read directly by
`00b_build_matrices.py` and placed under `data/raw/` by `download_public.sh`.

## Software that is cloned rather than installed

CHIRAL (step 06, phase concordance) comes from the scRITMO package, which is not on PyPI.
`download_public.sh` clones it into `data/raw/scritmo`; set `PYCHIRAL_SRC` to use another copy.
If it is absent, step 06 reports our own estimator and skips CHIRAL rather than failing.

## If NCBI answers with a CAPTCHA

The NCBI-generated counts are served by `www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov`, which intermittently replies with
an HTML challenge page instead of the file. `download_public.sh` now retries five times with
increasing waits and deletes any HTML it receives. GSE159984 is the one series that the pipeline
cannot do without: if NCBI keeps refusing, `T2D_ARCHS4_DL=1 bash download_public.sh` followed by
`python python/analyses/00c_archs4_counts.py` builds the same counts from ARCHS4 (a 62 GB
download).

## The time-matched control for the clamp (GSE40589)

Subcutaneous adipose biopsies taken 40 min before and 240 min after the start of an infusion that
begins at 08:00, with saline alone, with a euglycaemic hyperinsulinaemic clamp or with a
hyperglycaemic clamp (Tuvia et al., Diabetes 2021;70:1985). `download_public.sh` fetches the series
matrix and the platform table (`GPL6848_annot.txt`, taken from the SOFT file because GEO has no
curated annotation for that platform).

The series does not declare the participant. `28_clamp_vs_saline.py` pairs the two biopsies of an
experiment by the consecutive numbering of the sample titles and checks each pair with genes that
identify an individual (the Genetic and HLA sets of `python/lib/gene_sets.py`). A pair is excluded
from the primary analysis when its identity correlation falls below the 90th percentile of the
correlations between different pairs. The script also reports the analysis with every pair and
one that does not use the pairing at all.
